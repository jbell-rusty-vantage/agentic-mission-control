# Proof and experiments

**Current evidence routes through [NEXT-SESSION](NEXT-SESSION.md), 2026-10-29.** W0 is recorded
released; W1 is unreleased, with real rerun and repairs still required. This plan defines required
proof and does not certify completion. Historical Sprint V proof retains its original limitations.

## 1. Evidence and release rule

Every evidence row binds: criterion ID; fixture/version and exact objective; app/tenant/installation;
source/build/schema/contract/profile/skill/model pins; worker/Agent Server/backend/host identities;
mission/run/activation/attempt/generation; real versus doubled components; Transcript/output/plan/
receipt/native-readback locations and digests; observed result; pass/fail/blocked/unrun; reviewer;
repair issue and rerun lineage; token/cost/time/resource totals; release decision.

Keep private receipts and fixture data in ignored evidence storage, with safe locator/digest summaries
in docs/issues. A different agent reviews functional evidence. The integrator advances only when every
required criterion passes, including repairs and reruns; an invalidated earlier proof must be rerun at
the affected boundary. Tool success, parser acceptance, seeded catalog entries and doubles cannot stand
in for real model behavior, backend access, native effects or external host wake. Engineering release
does not confer scientific review or KS permitted-use authority.

Author concrete bounded W fixtures from the goals below at implementation start. They are small real
knowledge tasks with frozen inputs and objective criteria, not renamed F1/F2/F3; they may use tiny slices
of the same subjects (a few Generation Lab tests, one or two Qualia Life products, a handful of models)
(OC-D192). No further owner
interview is required for routine fixture details; preserve mandatory semantics and record engineering
choices. AI Engineer runs first, then Biotech at each applicable service boundary. An inapplicable cell
requires a narrow reason and does not certify untested application behavior.

## 9. F2 — Qualia Life products, ingredients and connected studies (WI-28)

Use a complex Biotech Stage Graph. Freeze every current supplement product/formula with observation date;
enumerate every ingredient/compound and produce a foundational report per product. Select **three** of the
most important qualifying connected case studies/trials, with exact connection and importance rationale.
Fewer verified qualifying studies is a visible shortfall, never fabricated evidence.

Recommended stage structure: inventory → exact product/label/formula join → shared ingredient research
partitions → study discovery/connection classification → independent KS evidence verification → grouped
per-product report assembly → assessment/coverage closure. Stages have distinct objectives, contracts,
skills/MCP allowlists. Distribute Tavily/Firecrawl/browser, PubMed, ClinicalTrials and KS evidence/ingest/
Biotech research to appropriate stages. Required verification provenance/status/findings reach the actual
final reports. Declare intent/apply requirements per stage; listing ingestion skills does not require
every artifact to be natively ingested.

Reuse BKS Product→ProductVariant→Package→LabelVersion and DECLARES_COMPONENT, IngredientMaterial and
substance/constituent relations; Study/registry/intervention/results and HAS_STUDY_ROLE distinguish
sponsor, funder, collaborator, material provider and ordinary studied compound. Inspect actual schema fit;
make source SDL/native projection migrations for missing needed invariants. Never infer sponsorship from
compound overlap. Preserve proprietary blends/unresolved printed ingredients and formula revisions.

Importance rubric is frozen before selection: direct product/formula applicability, study design and
quality, population/outcome relevance, source completeness and strength of company/ingredient connection.
Independent evaluator reviews all three selections and every product/ingredient coverage cell. Exercise
duplicate/shared ingredients, one failed product, source correction, unavailable trial, independently
closed product groups and verification disagreement without discarding unrelated successes.

## 10. F3 — Model ingestion then database-grounded ranking (WI-29/30)

Research up to 50 open-source and closed-source models, preferably 2025–2026. Do not require exactly 50
or silently turn the date preference into a hard filter. Preserve model/version/offering/license/openness,
benchmark/version/protocol/settings/units, observation/source dates and provenance. Missing/incomparable
scores stay explicit. Native model/benchmark entities and relationships must actually commit and pass
readback before ranking; intents alone fail.

Reuse AIDB corpus model/version/spec/offering/benchmark_run, knowledge benchmark_result and ranking
metric_observation representations. Required missing fields/relations receive owner migrations/types.
Ranking policy/run tables existing with read-only agent grants do not establish an autonomous writer.
Engineering choice: retain ranked choices/routing as typed output artifacts initially; add an admitted
native experiment writer only if selected by the fixture's explicit persistence contract. Never grant
agents broad table writes. Epoch AI is an example source, not a dependency.

WI-30 starts the ranking agent with a use case and policy, not the final candidate answers. It actually
retrieves from the ingested AIDB via qualified native/entity and applicable KS retrieval paths. Record
retrieved candidates, query/filters, evidence, dimensions, uncertainty, rank/rationale and routing outcome.
Embedding configuration and reranker version/degraded mode are explicit; a vector query alone does not
prove entity coverage. Non-benchmark dimensions require cited evidence or labelled judgment.

Experiment: freeze at least 6 varied use cases plus 2 held-out/adversarial cases covering quality,
cost/latency, context length, modality, deployment/license and uncertainty. Compare a declared baseline
and the multidimensional agent using the same data/pins. An independently curated relevant candidate set
supports recall@k ≥ 0.90 with k frozen per case before execution as the larger of 10 and the independently
curated relevant-set size, capped by the actual dataset population. Do not shrink relevance labels to
make the threshold pass. For zero-relevant-candidate cases, require correct abstention instead of recall.
All hard routing constraints must hold, all chosen factual claims must have
traceable support, and median independent usefulness is ≥4/5 under a frozen rubric. Record ranking
stability and nDCG/MRR where graded labels justify them; do not invent labels for convenience. Include
incomparable benchmarks, missing cost, unavailable candidates and abstention. A miss creates repair/rerun
work with the original case retained; the fixture is not accepted merely because SQL and prose succeeded.

## 11. Feedback and later architecture (WI-31)

During each active F1/F2/F3 issue, WI-26's evaluation mechanism produces classified findings with exact
evidence and owning layer. The active fixture owner immediately files and implements concrete fix issues
for code/schema/capability/contracts/prompts/workflow/evaluation gaps, links native blockers to the rerun,
and reruns versioned cases plus held-out regressions. Fixes never wait for downstream WI-31, which
aggregates final cross-fixture closure and handoff. Preserve
failed examples and before/after comparison; do not tune only to the demonstrated happy path. Stop within
admitted bounds with explicit unresolved blockers rather than unlimited optimization.

WI-31 closes only when each fixture's required criteria passes or an explicit owner scope decision is
recorded; an unresolved external source limitation is a truthful blocker, not silent completion. Produce
an evidence handoff listing durable capabilities, remaining gaps, candidate content/entities and their
quality. **Then** begin full AI Engineer education and Biotech knowledge/recommendation architecture.
Experimental reports and native data need not survive into final products.
