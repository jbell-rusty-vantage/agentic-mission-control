## Mental model restated

In the owner's terms: every repo carries `.sdlc/` and the skills, pinned; `node .sdlc/cli.mjs check` is green or the commit fails; a push with drifted modules stops and offers `propose`, which runs `agent -p --model grok-4.7-high` on the Cursor plan and commits the docs; the PR job repeats the gates, Cursor reviews, Linear moves, and green PRs merge themselves unless held or touching migrations; ideas are written in `platform/agentic-sdlc/ideas/` and `sdlc trace OVE-n` prints the chain back to the idea. Corrections the owner gave during the sitting: one implementation, not two (AS-D5); Cursor, not Claude, first (AS-D10); auto-merge allowed (AS-D16).
